# Eternally Yours - Business Overview

## Executive Summary
Eternally Yours is a premier, full-service event and wedding planning platform. It bridges the gap between event organizers, specialized vendors, and clients seeking a seamless, premium event planning experience. The platform provides a centralized hub where clients can discover, save, and book vendors ranging from photographers to caterers, while vendors and organizers can manage their business, track enquiries, and handle bookings.

## Core Value Proposition
- **For Clients:** A frictionless, visually stunning interface to explore curated event services. Clients can interact directly with organizers via live chat, utilize an AI-powered assistant for personalized vendor recommendations, and seamlessly book events.
- **For Organizers/Vendors:** A comprehensive "Vendor Portal" to list services, manage subscriptions, track real-time enquiries, and communicate with potential clients.
- **For Administrators:** A God-mode dashboard providing deep analytics on platform revenue, user engagement, and subscription management.

## Key Features & Business Model
1. **Vendor Subscriptions & Listings:** Vendors (Clients) request and manage subscriptions for premium access to list their services on the platform.
2. **Event & Booking Management:** Seamless ticket/booking creation for listed events.
3. **AI Chatbot Assistant:** An intelligent chatbot that helps users navigate the platform, pulling real-time vendor data and providing Google Maps integration for physical venues.
4. **Real-time Enquiries & Live Chat:** Users can directly message organizers. Opening a chat instantly tracks an "Enquiry" to convert passive browsing into active leads.
5. **Advanced Analytics:** Custom chart designers and global tracking of platform metrics to drive business decisions.

## Target Audience
- **Engaged Couples & Event Hosts:** Looking for high-quality, trusted vendors for weddings, receptions, and major celebrations.
- **Event Professionals:** Photographers, caterers, decorators, and venue managers seeking a dedicated platform to showcase their portfolios and manage client interactions.
